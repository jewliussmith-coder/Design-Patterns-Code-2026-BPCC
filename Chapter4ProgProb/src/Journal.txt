The Abstract Factory pattern guarantees compatible equipment because each factory creates a complete matching family of objects. The WarriorEquipmentFactory always creates a Sword and PlateArmor, while the MageEquipmentFactory always creates a Staff and Robe. The Enemy only needs to receive an EquipmentFactory, so it does not have to choose individual pieces of equipment. This prevents combinations such as a Mage using heavy Warrior armor and makes the equipment system easier to expand.
